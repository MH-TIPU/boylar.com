# boylar brand guide

## Logo

The logo is a lowercase `b` in a rounded square, plus a bold `boylar` wordmark.

| Version | Use |
|---|---|
| Mark | App icons, favicons, social profile pictures, anywhere square |
| Horizontal lockup | Website header, letterhead, email signature, business cards |
| Stacked lockup | Narrow spaces, merchandise, signage, stamps |
| Wordmark | Where the mark already appears elsewhere on the same surface |

## Colour

| Name | Hex | RGB | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purple | `#534AB7` | 83, 74, 183 | Primary. The mark, links, buttons |
| Purple deep | `#3C3489` | 60, 52, 137 | Text on light purple, hover states |
| Purple light | `#7F77DD` | 127, 119, 221 | Accents, charts, illustrations |
| Purple tint | `#EEEDFE` | 238, 237, 254 | Section backgrounds, cards |
| Black | `#111111` | 17, 17, 17 | Body text |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 | Reversed logo, backgrounds |

CMYK for print: Purple is approximately C 78 M 79 Y 0 K 0. Confirm with the printer before a large run.

## Typography

Poppins, SIL Open Font License, free for commercial use. Included in `fonts/`.

- Wordmark: Poppins Bold, tracking −2%
- Letter in the mark: Poppins Medium
- Headings: Poppins SemiBold
- Body text: Poppins Regular, or a system sans if page weight matters

The logo files have the letters converted to outlines. They render identically without Poppins installed.

## Clear space

Keep empty space around the logo equal to the height of the `b` in the wordmark. Nothing sits inside that margin.

## Minimum sizes

| Asset | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Mark | 16 px | 6 mm |
| Horizontal lockup | 90 px wide | 25 mm wide |
| Stacked lockup | 60 px wide | 18 mm wide |

## Backgrounds

- On white and light backgrounds: the purple version
- On purple and dark backgrounds: the white version
- On photographs: the white version, over a darkened area of the image
- Single-colour printing, embroidery, engraving, stamps: the black or white knockout version

## Rules

- The logo is never stretched, rotated, or skewed.
- The letter is never recoloured to anything other than white or purple.
- The corner radius is never changed.
- No drop shadows, gradients, glows, or outlines are applied.
- The wordmark is never retyped in a different font. Use the supplied files.
- `boylar` is always lowercase.

## Trademark

These files are original work. Registering `boylar` as a trademark in Bangladesh is handled by the Department of Patents, Designs and Trademarks. Run a search before printing signage or large stock.
